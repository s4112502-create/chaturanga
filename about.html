The Ancient War Game of Kurukshetra

Long before modern chess spread across the world, ancient India played Chaturanga—literally meaning "four limbs"—representing the four divisions of a royal army: Infantry, Cavalry, Elephants, and Chariots. Chaturanga: Kurukshetra 3D brings that battlefield to life with 3D warriors, dynamic camera angles, and cinematic combat animations.

Meet the Six Warriors

♔ Raja (King)

The sovereign leader crowned with the royal Chatra umbrella. Moves one square in any direction. Protect your Raja at all costs—if he falls in checkmate, the war is lost.

♕ Mantri (Queen)

The supreme commander wielding the Khanda sword. Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally across the battlefield.

♖ Ratha (Rook)

The armored war chariot with spoked wheels and temple canopy. Charges in straight lines along any rank or file.

♗ Gaja (Bishop)

The armored war elephant carrying a royal Howdah. Sweeps diagonally across the battlefield to ambush enemy flanks.

♘ Ashva (Knight)

The swift warhorse of the cavalry. Leaps in an L-shape (two squares in one direction and one perpendicular) and can jump over other warriors.

♙ Padati (Pawn)

Foot soldiers armed with spear and shield. March forward one square (two on their opening move) and capture diagonally. Reaching the 8th rank promotes them to a higher commander.

Controls & Tips

• Move Warriors: Click any highlighted gold or crimson piece (your army) to see legal move dots and red capture rings, then click the target square.
• Camera Control: Drag with your mouse (or finger) to orbit the 3D battlefield, scroll to zoom, or switch instantly between Classic 3D, War Angle, and Top 2D.
• AI Opponents: Challenge Shishya (Easy), Yoddha (Medium), or Acharya Drona (Hard) as either the Pandavas or Kauravas.
